# Project Roadmap

## Phase 1: Tenant Payment Flow
**Context:** Tenants need a way to pay the rent and utilities assigned to them.
**Goal:** A working Tenant Dashboard where users can log in, see pending invoices, and execute a simulated or real Razorpay payment.

## Phase 2: Maintenance Loop
**Context:** Things break in co-living spaces.
**Goal:** Tenants can submit tickets; Owners can view and resolve them, optionally passing on charges.

## Phase 3: Move-Out & Ledger 
**Context:** When a lease ends, deposits must be settled.
**Goal:** A robust termination flow that frees up the bed and computes the final financial settlement.
